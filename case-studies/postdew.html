THE PRODUCT

The problem

AI-written posts were losing the human voice.

AI-assisted LinkedIn posts often sound repetitive, overly polished, and obviously machine-generated. PostDew was designed to help users turn rough thoughts into more natural posts while preserving their original meaning and voice.

What I built

One product. Every layer.

Product thinking, application engineering, integrations, infrastructure, and launch—owned end to end.

01

Django REST backend and React frontend.

02

OpenAI-powered generation designed around natural voice.

Business Systems

Subscription billing with DodoPayments and analytics with PostHog.

Production

Error monitoring and deployment across Hetzner and Vercel.
